Transportation Options

Select the most convenient travel option based on your location and work schedule.

directions_bus

RapidKL Bus

Board bus 821 or 851 and disembark at KL 1022 MATRADE South bus stop. MITEC is approximately a 4-minute walk from the bus bay.

train

MRT Line

Disembark at Semantan Station (KG14) and continue to MITEC via a short 10-minute taxi or e-hailing ride.

subway

KTM Komuter

Disembark at Segambut Station and transfer to MITEC using taxi or e-hailing service.

local_taxi

Taxi & E-Hailing

Request drop-off directly at North Entrance or South Entrance from anywhere across Kuala Lumpur or Petaling Jaya.

directions_car

Private Vehicle

Conveniently accessible via major highways including DUKE, NKVE, SPRINT Highway, and Jalan Kuching.

tips_and_updates

Pro Travel Tip for Shift Staff

During peak exhibition days and large-scale conventions, traffic along Jalan Dutamas can build up quickly. We recommend arriving at least 20–30 minutes prior to your shift briefing. For public transport commuters, taking the MRT to Semantan followed by Grab offers the most reliable transit time.
