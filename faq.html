Frequently Asked Questions
Welcome! Please head directly to the Security Post at the West Gate Entrance (Loading Bay Zone). Inform the security team that you are a part-time/casual staff member to collect your physical voucher.

Important: As soon as you receive your voucher, please log in to the Sign-In Portal immediately to register your voucher number.
Parking is completely free of charge for casual staff! Simply proceed to the West Gate, and our security officers on duty will happily guide you to the designated complimentary parking area.
Once your voucher is successfully registered, head up to the Linen Room on Level 1A to collect your uniform. We highly recommend contacting your assigned Supervisor, Person-in-Charge (PIC), or team members beforehand so you know exactly where to report for duty.
Payout delays are usually caused by one of the following reasons:
  • Incomplete Bank Details: Forgetting to update or verify your bank account details in the portal profile.
  • Delayed Voucher Submission: Not handing in your physical voucher slip to the security post immediately after completing your shift.
Please ensure both actions are completed promptly to guarantee on-time processing!
To ensure fairness and accurate tracking, salary adjustments or deductions are applied based on the system logs in these situations:
  • Missing Portal Clocking: Failing to Sign-In or Sign-Out your voucher via the online portal. Please note that department-specific manual attendance lists or physical QR codes do not substitute this requirement.
  • Lateness: Signing in to the portal more than 5 minutes past your scheduled shift start time.
